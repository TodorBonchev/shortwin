# M5 T-TRACE (pump curve)

Instruction-trace entries (every top-level instruction, ComputeBudget included, plus every CPI at any depth; ARCH §6.2) of the worst-case curve transactions of the release short.win on the dumped pump, pump-fees and PumpSwap programs, measured on LiteSVM 0.16.0 with every invariant hook after every instruction; the limit is `MAX_INSTRUCTION_TRACE_LENGTH = 64`. The peak stack height is the deepest CPI level reached (1 = top level; the mainnet limit is 5, ARCH §6.3); the CU is the whole transaction's (the two ComputeBudget instructions cost 150 each), within ARCH §6.1's limit for it. Written by `M5_TRACE_WRITE=1 cargo test --manifest-path tests/bench/m5_cu/Cargo.toml --test t_trace` (README.md); the exact entries of each row are also asserted (their composition is pinned in `tests/litesvm/tests/trace_pump_it.rs`).

Sizes (T-SIZE preview, ARCH §6.4): the legacy wire size (no lookup table) and the v0 wire size through the protocol ALT (A28 with its pump part: pump, PumpSwap and pump-fees, their statics and the live recipient lists with their WSOL ATAs; `m5_cu_bench::protocol_alt_addresses`), each with its account count; LiteSVM enforces neither. Every client and keeper transaction must fit v0 + ALT (≤ 1,232 B, ≤ 64 accounts). Bold: above 1,232 B. The full T-SIZE on the curve (the largest extras set, a separate fee payer, both tables, executed as v0) is `tests/litesvm/tests/tx_size_pump_it.rs`.

A position past the curve's `rT` exits in one transaction (pump v3's synthetic migration, the synthetic rows). The keeper's `migrate_v2` is measured after such a completion (`pc ≠ 0`) and after a third party's exact one (`pc = 0`; until pump-final P6 the straddle bundle's tx2). The bundle's helper rows (tx1, the `complete_curve` helper) are retired with the helper (pump-final D2).
