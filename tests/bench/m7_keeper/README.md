# m7_keeper — keeper cost-model inputs, batch sizes, load and the PumpSwap 4-extras T-SIZE (M7)

Measured inputs of ECONOMICS §5.8's keeper cost model (ARCHITECTURE §9 M7 "keeper cost
measurement … feeding back into ECON §5.8"; §8.2 T-SIZE's batch sizes; §7.1 duties 7, 9, 11) and
the measured answer to OWNER-M7-1 (the PumpSwap open with the largest extras set over A28). No
estimate: every figure is the **real keeper's own transaction** (`services/keeper`), built by its
own builder (`shortwin_services_common::tx`: `⌈simulated × 1.1⌉` compute units, the priority price
set), executed in-process by LiteSVM with the harness's invariant hooks after every instruction —
on the mock venue (the test-venue build) and on the mainnet forks of Raydium AMM v4 (Fartcoin),
CPMM (USELESS), PumpSwap (the `mature` pool; `Legacy261`, `Mature` and `Cashback` for T-SIZE) and
the pump curve (the `fresh` coin). This directory is output only; the code is in
`services/keeper-litesvm/tests/scenarios/{m7_cost,pamm_alt}.rs`.

| file | what | generated by |
|---|---|---|
| `results.md`, `results.json` | CU per instruction and venue (`crank_price` alone, `liquidate`, `expire`, `sol_settle` arming / keeping and settling: consumed, requested, bytes); lamports per exit at 0 / 1,000 / 10,000 / 100,000 µlamports/CU; the global lines (`set_coverage_pause`, `crank_sol_usd` and its calls a day); cranks per transaction per venue and for a mix (v1, v0 through the protocol ALT, v0 through the protocol ALT plus a table per market, v0 bare); the keeper's cadence (quiet, near a threshold, armed, a price burst, a genuine move, a liquidity trigger, a PumpSwap warm-up); cost per market-day per venue at a share f of the day near a threshold, unbatched / v1 k = 6 / v0; load at 50 and 200 markets and the per-second capacity it implies over JSON-RPC | `m7_cost::m7_keeper_cost_inputs` (`#[ignore]`: a bench) |
| `tsize_pumpswap.md`, `tsize_pumpswap.json` | the PumpSwap transactions with the largest (4-entry) extras set through (a) A28 as ARCH §6.4 enumerates it, (b) the table `shortwin-build-alt create` builds, and each plus the market's own table (`shortwin-build-alt market`); the market table's size and rent; the keeper's liquidation sent on v0 through (b) + the market's table | `pamm_alt::the_pumpswap_four_extras_transactions_through_the_keepers_tables` (runs in the default suite; writes with `M7_COST_WRITE=1`) |

The JSON schemas are `shortwin.m7.keeper_cost/1` and `shortwin.m7.tsize_pumpswap/1`; lamport
figures follow exactly from the measured limits: `5,000 × signatures + ⌈requested CU × price /
10⁶⌉`. `builds` records the SHA-256 of the two program builds the run loaded.

## Regenerate

```sh
anchor build --tools-version v1.57 --arch v3 --ignore-keys && scripts/build-test-venue.sh
M7_COST_WRITE=1 cargo test --manifest-path services/Cargo.toml --release --locked \
  -p shortwin-keeper-litesvm --test scenarios -- --include-ignored --nocapture m7_cost:: pamm_alt::
```

`M7_COST_FULL=1` runs the quiet cadence for an hour instead of 15 minutes (it is periodic: the
day's figure is the same). The same env overrides as the scenario suite apply (`SHORTWIN_SO`,
`SHORTWIN_TEST_VENUE_SO`, `MOCK_CPAMM_SO`).

## What it gates

- every measured limit is exactly `⌈consumed × 1.1⌉`, every transaction within 4,096 B;
- v0 through the protocol ALT holds 4 AMM v4 / 4 CPMM / 3 PumpSwap / 5 curve cranks (ARCH §6.4,
  §7.1 item 7), a v1 batch of 6 fits every venue and the mix, a table per market never holds
  fewer, within 1,232 B and 64 accounts;
- the cadence: a quiet market every 30 s, a market near a threshold every second, the PumpSwap
  warm-up 8 cranks, an isolated liquidity trigger 1–10 extra cranks, duty 11 one crank per feed
  update; every due market observed in its second at 50 and 200 markets;
- T-SIZE (OWNER-M7-1): the first-time borrower's open with a separate fee payer and the largest
  extras set is over 1,232 B (by ≤ 24 B) only through A28 as enumerated, and fits through (b) and
  through either table plus the market's; the borrower paying its own fee, and the keeper's
  liquidation and `[crank_price, liquidate]` (the keeper is its own fee payer), fit every table;
  the open executes through (b) and (a) + the market's table; the market table's lamports are the
  rent-exempt minimum of mainnet's Rent (5,080 lamports per byte, ARCH V5).
