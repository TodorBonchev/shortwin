# short.win known issues register

This is the one list of what short.win knowingly allows. Auditors should not report these as
findings unless the stated bound is wrong or can be exceeded. Each row gives:

- **Bound**: the numeric limit, as measured or simulated in the cited section.
- **Decision**: the owner decision or open owner item that covers the row.
- **Status**:
  - **accepted**: the owner recorded it in SPEC §14 or ARCH §10 (the AI review's rows: in its
    README's "Decisions for the owner"), with the date;
  - **disclosed**: built and disclosed, but the owner has not yet accepted it (the item is
    named);
  - **open**: the owner has to choose, and the build default is in force until then;
  - **by design**;
  - **fixed**: a finding fixed in the program before the audit tag, or after it in the AI
    review's fix batch (§7: built with its regression test and through a local run of the CI
    gate, but not yet through CI or an external fix review; the ≥ 24 h fuzz run waived by the
    owner for this batch, 2026-10-13); the row stays for its residual, if any.

The register merges these sources:

- ARCH §10 O19 (the disclosed residuals) and the M7 owner items;
- SPEC §14;
- the residuals in ARCH §2.6, §3.x, §6.6 and §8.5, and those in the changelog rows;
- ECON §8.2, §9.3–§9.5, §12.2–§12.12 and §17;
- the M10 findings, the owner decisions of 2026-10-02 and 2026-10-03 (SPEC §14, ARCH §10) and
  the M10 work-package reports (§6 below);
- the AI review of v1.0.0 (`docs/audit/ai-review/`: its findings AIR-01 … AIR-24, the triage of
  its leads, the fixes and the owner's decisions of 2026-10-12 and 2026-10-13; §7 below);
- pump's 2026-10-08 … 10-13 redeploys and short.win's v3 curve adapter (the design
  `pump-final`, release 1.0.1; the owner's decisions D1 (a) … D4 of 2026-10-13; AUDIT.md
  "Addendum: pump v3"): KI-77 and the PUMP-2026-10-13-* rows of §6.

The numbers are copied from those sections on the M10 final tree (the commit docs/AUDIT.md §0
names). They are not re-derived. When a design doc changes, its row here changes in the same
commit (docs/AUDIT.md §12). Rows KI-01 … KI-51 date from the M10 skeleton (`e857a2a`); rows
KI-52 … KI-68 were added in M10. Rows KI-69 … KI-76 come from the AI review of v1.0.0: their

(`leads.md` L-2 carries the erratum). KI-77 and the PUMP-2026-10-13-* rows (2026-10-16) take
their figures from the pump re-pin's gates (`tests/fixtures/README.md`), the design's survey and
